Get started · about a minute

Three steps, done once

1

Add the bookmark

Cellblock Helper

Drag the purple button up to your bookmarks bar (favorites bar in Edge).

Cellblock Helper
No bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B.
2

Open the game

neopets.com/games/cellblock

Go to Cellblock on Neopets and continue or start a game.

Open the game ↗
3

Click the bookmark

Once, on the game page. The square to play glows purple with a label. Click it, then press Make Move. It keeps going for every move and every game until you close the tab.

Purple: play hereThe card says why.
Make MoveGreen: press it nowYou picked the right square.
Red dashesYou picked a different square. Click the purple one.

After a win, press Next game of the match on the helper's card. The game's own Play Again button starts the match over from the first game.

The card in the corner of the game says what the move does. Its × puts the page back to normal. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Windows and Mac.
